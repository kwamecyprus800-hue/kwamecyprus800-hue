# ABOUT ME – MY BIOGRAPHY

My name is **CFN Boateng Tetteh Ebenezer**. I was born on **2nd September 2001** to Rev. Godsplan Tetteh Boateng and the late Joyce Tetteh Boateng. I am a 25-year-old Ghanaian who believes in discipline, hard work, dedication, and continuous self-development.

My educational journey began at **Kibi Covenant Preparatory School**, where I received my basic education. After successfully completing my primary education, I proceeded to **Attafuah Senior High Technical School**, where I furthered my studies. I completed my Senior High School education in 2019.

After completing school, I had the opportunity to work at an **MTN office** for some time, where I gained valuable experience and developed my interpersonal and professional skills.

In 2023, I was enlisted into the **Ghana Armed Forces**, marking an important milestone in my life. Through determination, commitment, and perseverance, I successfully completed my military training and passed out in 2024. I currently serve as a unit personnel of the **Electrical and Mechanical Engineers** in the Ghana Armed Forces. I am also undertaking a course at **DIT** to further enhance my knowledge, technical abilities, and professional competence.

Beyond my military career and education, I am passionate about music. I am an instrumentalist who plays a variety of musical instruments. Music is one of my greatest interests and a way through which I express my creativity.

In my leisure time, I enjoy watching football, even though I cannot personally play the game. Interestingly, I do not have a particular favourite food because I enjoy different kinds of meals.

I consider myself a person who is always willing to learn, grow, and embrace new challenges. My journey so far has taught me the importance of patience, discipline, resilience, and determination. I aspire to continue developing myself professionally and personally while making meaningful contributions to my unit, the Ghana Armed Forces, and society as a whole.

**My story is still unfolding, and I remain committed to becoming a better version of myself every day.**
